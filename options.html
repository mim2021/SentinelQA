SentinelQA settings

Looks up the scanned domain's registration date (rdap.org) and cross-checks its IP with Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS. Only the hostname you scan is sent, only when you open the popup.

When set, the full URL you scan is sent to Google Safe Browsing. The key is stored unencrypted in this browser profile's extension storage; use a key restricted to the Safe Browsing API.
